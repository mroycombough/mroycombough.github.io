The Carolina Hurricanes

For the Hometown Remix, the Carolina Hurricanes organization thought it would be a great idea to create a jersey that celebrates Raleigh, North Carolina, and its nickname, the "City of Oaks." To go more into detail, Raleigh earned this nickname because of the many oak trees found throughout the city. The jersey also celebrates North Carolina's culture by featuring the team's mascot, Stormy, a pig that represents the state's famous pork and barbecue traditions. The main color of this jersey is storm gray, which covers most of the jersey, including the chest, back, shoulders, and sleeves. The second color is bright red, which is used for the sleeve stripes, parts of the center design, and other details. The third color is black, which appears in smaller design accents and the mascot's clothing. For the center design, instead of using the traditional Hurricanes logo, the jersey features Stormy in the "Struttin' Stormy" design, wearing a CANES bucket hat, shirt, shorts, and sneakers. This is the first time Stormy has appeared as the main front crest on a Hurricanes jersey. On the shoulders, the jersey features special patches inspired by North Carolina's state flag and the dome of the North Carolina State Capitol building. The red stripes on the sleeves contain debossed oak leaves, representing Raleigh's nickname, the City of Oaks. Finally, along the bottom hem of the jersey, there is a design featuring the Raleigh skyline, the Lenovo Center, trees, and the words "City of Oaks." Overall, this jersey connects to Raleigh by celebrating its oak trees, local culture, city architecture, and hockey history while bringing North Carolina's identity into the Hurricanes' uniform..

5 Fun Facts About the Carolina Hurricanes Hometown Remix Jersey

  1. Stormy is featured as the main chest design instead of the Hurricanes' traditional logo.
  2. The jersey includes a special design inspired by North Carolina's state flag.
  3. The shoulder patches take inspiration from the dome of the North Carolina State Capitol building.
  4. The bottom hem features a miniature design of Raleigh's skyline.
  5. The jersey celebrates Raleigh's nickname, the "City of Oaks," through special oak leaf patterns.
