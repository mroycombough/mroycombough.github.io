The Chicago Blackhawks

For the Hometown Remix, the Chicago Blackhawks organization thought it would be a great idea to create a jersey that celebrates Chicago's history of progress, ambition, and its famous St. Patrick's Day tradition of dyeing the Chicago River green. To go more into detail, the year 2026 marks 50 years since the Blackhawks first featured the famous motto "I Will the Spirit of Chicago" on their home jersey. The main color of this jersey is bright green, which covers almost the entire jersey, including the chest, back, shoulders, and sleeves. This green also connects to Chicago's tradition of dyeing the Chicago River green every St. Patrick's Day. The second color is gold, which is used for the shoulder patches and other small details. The third colors are black and white, which are used for the player numbers and sleeve stripes. For the center logo, the jersey features the Blackhawks' current traditional logo. On both shoulders, there are gold patches, while each sleeve features four debossed six-pointed stars inspired by the four stars on Chicago's city flag. The collar also features a lace-up design inspired by Blackhawks jerseys from the 1960s. Finally, the inside bottom hem features the words "I Will the Spirit of Chicago," celebrating the motto's 50th anniversary. Overall, this jersey connects to Chicago by celebrating its traditions, hockey history, city flag, and its famous spirit of progress and ambition.
