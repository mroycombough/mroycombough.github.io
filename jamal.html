The Calgary Flames

For the Hometown Remix, the Calgary Flames organization thought it would be a great idea to create a jersey that celebrates the Scotiabank Saddledome, the arena that has been home to the Flames since 1983. To go more into detail, this jersey was designed as a tribute to the Saddledome during its final season, making it a special way to remember the building and its history. The main color of this jersey is bright red, which covers most of the jersey, including the chest, back, shoulders, and sleeves. The second color is metallic gold, called "88 Gold," which is used for the center logo, sleeve stripes, and other details. The jersey also features white accents alongside the gold stripes. For the center logo, the jersey features the famous Flaming "C" logo in metallic gold, with a special texture inspired by the ceiling grid of the Saddledome. On the sleeves, the gold stripes feature debossed patterns inspired by the arena's iconic circular porthole windows. Another special detail is the bottom hem, where the stripes follow the shape of the Saddledome's famous curved roofline. The player numbers also feature a texture inspired by the arena's shutters. Finally, along the inside bottom hem, the years 1983 and 2027 represent when the Saddledome opened and when it will close. Overall, this jersey connects to Calgary by celebrating the Saddledome's architecture, its 44 years of history, and the memories it has created for Flames fans. The raised gold grid pattern on the centre c of this jersey also makes it look like an Eggo waffle because of its small square-shaped details. It gives the jersey a unique texture that stands out from the red background
