The Colorado Avalanche

For the Hometown Remix, the Colorado Avalanche organization thought it would be a great idea to create a jersey that celebrates Colorado’s famous Rocky Mountains and snowy landscapes. The main color of this jersey is white, which represents the snow that covers the Colorado mountains during the winter. The second colors are different shades of blue, which appear throughout the jersey in the mountain-inspired designs. The third color is metallic silver, which adds a frosty, glistening look to the sleeves and details around the logos. For the center logo, the jersey features a large letter “C” inspired by the Colorado state flag. Inside the “C” is a circular shape that represents the gold circle found in the state flag’s design, helping connect the jersey to Colorado. The logo and player numbers on the back also feature a mountain elevation pattern made up of graduating shades of blue, representing the different heights and ridges of the Rocky Mountains. The shoulders feature Rocky Mountain details, while the traditional Avalanche logo appears on the shoulder patches with silver accents. Overall, this jersey connects to Colorado by celebrating its mountains, snowy scenery, state flag, and natural beauty.
