The Anaheim Ducks

For the Hometown remix the Anaheim Ducks organization thought that it would be a great idea to bring back and connect with their orignal jersey from the disney movie "The Mighty Ducks" (1990).To go more into detail the ducks adopted this jersey because the mighty ducks was the first ever Disney hockey movie. And the disney headquarters is located on Anaheim California where This NHL team was introduced. This jersey has the main color of jade deep green as the base and for the chest. This jersey also has yellow for the players names, back numbers, side numbers, neck lining for their jersey, side shoulder stripes, and the bottom stripes for their jersey. The last color that this jersey features is deep purple. The areas that have deep purple on it are the exact same places where the gold is, except that insted of one layer like the gold it is on the top part and the bottem part of the gold spaces.For the centre logo of this jersey, it has a yellow triangular cone, in which it is outlined with a thin layer of black and purple. The inside of the jersey logo has two golden hockey sticks with with tape at the bottem. These sticks are located on the left side and right side of a cartoon-style duck face old-school hockey goalie mask (featuring bright glowing eyes and a fierce expression) This mask also is white.
