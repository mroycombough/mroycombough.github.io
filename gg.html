The Buffalo Sabres.

For the Hometown Remix, the Buffalo Sabres organization thought it would be a great idea to create a jersey that celebrates Buffalo's history as the "City of Light." To go more into detail, Buffalo earned this nickname because it became one of the first American cities to have widespread electric street lighting. This jersey connects to that history by featuring lightning bolts, stars, and special designs inspired by electricity and Niagara Falls. The main color of this jersey is deep royal blue, which covers most of the jersey, including the chest, back, shoulders, and sleeves. The second color is metallic gold, which is used for the outline of the center logo, the shoulder details, and the stripes on the sleeves and bottom of the jersey. The third color is white, which appears in the stripes alongside the gold and in the lightning bolt and star designs. For the center logo, the jersey features the famous Buffalo Goathead logo, which has a fierce buffalo head with a bright red eye and a gold outline. On the shoulders, the jersey features the team's letter "B" logo intertwined with an electrified sabre. Another special detail is the debossed lightning bolts and stars pressed into the fabric of the sleeves, representing Buffalo's history with electricity. The players' names and numbers also feature custom lettering inspired by the original turbine equipment at Niagara Falls, connecting the jersey to the area's history of hydroelectric power. Finally, the inside bottom hem of the jersey features the word "BUFFALO" displayed between stars. Overall, this jersey connects to Buffalo by celebrating its history as the City of Light, its connection to Niagara Falls and hydroelectric power, and the Sabres' identity through its famous logos and traditional colors..
