The Columbus Blue Jackets

For the Hometown Remix, the Columbus Blue Jackets organization thought it would be a great idea to create a jersey that celebrates Columbus’s history, architecture, and passionate hockey fans. The main color of this jersey is solid grey, which covers the body and creates a clean background for the other design details to stand out. The shoulders feature navy blue panels, while navy and lighter blue stripes appear along the sleeves and bottom of the jersey. White is used to outline the large cannon crest on the front and the player numbers, making them stand out against the grey background. Silver is another important color because it represents strength and resilience, connecting to the cannon and the spirit of the team and city. The cannon crest is especially important because it connects to Columbus’s Civil War history. The Blue Jackets' name honors Ohio’s contributions to the Union during the Civil War, and the cannon also connects to the team's tradition of celebrating goals at Nationwide Arena. One of the most interesting details is the arched design on the sleeves, inspired by Columbus’s nickname, “The Arch City,” and the historic arches along High Street, particularly in the Short North area. Behind the sleeve numbers is a debossed filigree pattern inspired by local history, with additional decorative details on the shoulders. Finally, the bottom hem features an italicized “5th Line” graphic, representing the Blue Jackets’ loyal fans and their importance to the team. Overall, this jersey connects to Columbus by celebrating its Civil War history, architecture, team traditions, and fan community.
