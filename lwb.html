The Boston Bruins

The Boston Bruins For the Hometown Remix, the Boston Bruins organization thought it would be a great idea to create a jersey called the "Varsity Sweater," which connects to Boston's strong college hockey culture and the Bruins' original uniform from 1924. To go more into detail, Boston is home to several famous college hockey programs, including Boston University, Boston College, Harvard, and Northeastern. These universities have helped make Boston one of the most important hockey cities in the United States. This jersey celebrates that connection by using a design inspired by traditional college sports uniforms. The main color of this jersey is dark brown, which covers most of the jersey and brings back the Bruins' original uniform color from their first season in 1924. The second color is golden yellow, which is used for the large "BRUINS" lettering across the chest, the player numbers, the shoulder patch, and the stripes on the sleeves and around the bottom of the jersey. The third color is white or cream, which appears alongside the gold stripes on the sleeves and bottom of the jersey. For the front of the jersey, instead of using the traditional spoked-B logo, it features the word "BRUINS" in large, arched, gold collegiate-style lettering. Directly underneath the lettering is a large player number, inspired by traditional university hockey uniforms. The collar also features a lace-up design, giving the jersey an old-fashioned hockey sweater appearance. On both sleeves, there are double stripes that continue the varsity theme. Another special detail is the shield-shaped shoulder patch featuring a collegiate-style letter "B." The jersey also brings back front-side player numbers, a feature the Bruins have not used since the 1940s. On the back, the players' names appear above specially designed numbers. One final hometown detail is located along the inside bottom hem ofthe jersey. It features a red-brick pattern inspired by Boston's famous Freedom Trail, a historic walking route that passes by important landmarks throughoutthe city. Overall, this jersey connects to Boston by celebrating its college hockey culture, historic architecture, andthe Bruins' original hockey history while combining all three into one unique design..
