Nhl Hometown remix jerseys and the meaning behind them

Why did the Nhl intoduce this

The reason why Gary Bettman ( the NHL commissioner) and Fanatics (a jersey company) introduced this new jersey type was to gain more money in sales and to let teams have a jersey that tells a story about their hometowns. To go further, an explanation around 1-3 years ago Gary Bettman and his team met with Dom Fillion (Fanatics' vice president of creative, design, and innovation) and his team. And proposed an idea in which the NHL would team up with Fanatics and make a new Jersey type for all 32 NHL teams. And that by making and selling these jerseys, the amount of revenue for both companies would skyrocket. NHL fans would love new jersey styles. After Fanatics accepted the idea, they started working on the jerseys. Then, fast-forward to Nov. 2, 2026: Dom Fillion, Fanatics, Gary Bettman, and the NHL stated that the jersey would be called the Hometown Remix and that "We didn't necessarily want to create a platform that was only looking backward and telling old stories, but really allowing this platform to create something new. This is how Hometown Remix came to life—it connects the team, the city, and the fans together" and that “The team's brand is still at the core of the play. What are those elements that are really hyperlocal that only the fan in your city would know?' It was a great way to jump-start the conversation.”
